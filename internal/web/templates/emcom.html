Operational readiness levels

To launch crisis communications in a way that is efficient and adequate to the situation, the following operational readiness levels apply:

{{range .Levels}}
level {{.Level}} {{.Name}}

{{.Description}}

{{end}}
  • PMR and CB monitoring uses devices that meet the requirements of the given mode of communication. A channel being listed in the communications plan does not authorize using any amateur radio on it.
  • Activating level 2 or 3 means switching to directed-net operation.
  • Level 3 means the network can operate around the clock in shifts, provided enough operators, power, transport, backup communications and logistics are available. It does not mean uninterrupted work of a single operator.
  • The club may on its own start internal monitoring, increased readiness and a control radio network. Deploying operators at the premises of external entities and performing tasks for public administration, services or other units requires a request, consent or confirmed arrangement with an authorized representative of that entity.

Raising a network above level 0 publishes a severe communication and dispatches it through the group channels per the routing matrix. Returning to level 0 closes the network's active communication.
